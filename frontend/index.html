Help

      

        
Inviting
Use the add-person icon to copy the meeting link. Guests open it, enter a name and join.

        
Roles
The host leads the meeting and controls who is in it. Producers get the production tools. Guests manage their own camera and microphone.

        
Host / Producer view
This only changes which tools you see. It never changes what you are allowed to do.

        
Chat
Chat reaches people who are in the meeting right now. It is not saved.

        
Notes
Notes, agenda and moments are private and saved only in this browser.
